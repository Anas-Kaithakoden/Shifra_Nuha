# Shifra Nuha Technologies — Startup Structure & Website Brief

## 1. Company Positioning

**Company:** Shifra Nuha Technologies

### Recommended positioning

> **We help businesses start, build, grow, and automate.**

Alternative:

> **Everything your business needs to launch and grow, under one roof.**

Shifra Nuha Technologies should be positioned as a **business launch, digital growth, and technology partner**, rather than only an accounting firm, web-development agency, or marketing agency.

The company combines:
- Business setup and professional services through qualified partners
- Branding and digital presence
- Marketing and lead generation
- AI, automation, and custom technology

The long-term goal can be:

**Services → learn customer problems → standardize → automate → productize → SaaS**

---

# 2. Service Structure

Organize the business into four major service areas.

## START — Business Setup & Compliance

Professional/regulated services should be delivered or supervised by the appropriate qualified professionals.

Services can include:

- Company incorporation
- LLP incorporation
- Partnership setup
- GST registration and related services
- MSME/Udyam registration
- FSSAI registration where applicable
- Import Export Code (IEC)
- Accounting
- Bookkeeping
- Income-tax related services
- GST compliance/returns
- Audit coordination
- Payroll support
- Project reports
- Financial projections
- Business documentation
- Trademark application coordination
- Other applicable registrations

### Professional partners

- **CA:** Accounting, taxation, GST, audit-related professional work
- **CS:** Company/LLP incorporation and applicable corporate compliance
- **Lawyer:** Legal agreements, contracts, legal matters
- **Trademark/IP professional:** Trademark/IP-related work where appropriate

Shifra Nuha Technologies can manage the customer relationship and coordination while the appropriate professional performs regulated professional work.

The company should not remain only a generic service agency.

   ↓

   ↓
Repeated work
   ↓
Standardize
   ↓
Automate
   ↓
Productize
   ↓

- Candidate collection
- CV parsing

# Important Implementation Rules

1. Do not invent business contact information.
2. Do not invent testimonials.
3. Do not invent customer logos.
4. Do not invent pricing unless explicitly provided.
5. Do not claim professional qualifications that were not provided.
6. Do not claim that Shifra Nuha Technologies itself performs regulated professional work where qualified professionals are required.
7. Make the content easy to edit later.
8. Use reusable components.
9. Keep the first version simple.
10. Prioritize a polished user experience over feature count.
11. Ensure the site builds successfully.
12. Fix responsive layout problems before finishing.
13. Check accessibility basics:
   - semantic HTML
   - labels for forms
   - keyboard navigation
   - readable contrast
   - alt text where images are used
14. Add basic SEO:
   - title
   - meta description
   - semantic headings
   - Open Graph metadata if appropriate
15. Do not use fake statistics such as "500+ clients".
16. Do not use fake reviews or testimonials.
17. Do not use copyrighted stock images unless they are properly available for use.
