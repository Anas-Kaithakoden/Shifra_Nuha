# Shifra Nuha Technologies — Website, Ads & SEO Strategy Brief

## Purpose

Update the existing Shifra Nuha Technologies website to match the company's actual initial go-to-market strategy.

The initial strategy is focused:

> Acquire business owners through affordable business registration and professional business-service advertisements, convert them through WhatsApp/calls, and then cross-sell additional services such as websites, branding, marketing and automation.

The website should therefore be a **conversion-focused business-registration and professional-services website**, while still presenting Shifra Nuha Technologies as a company capable of supporting customers after registration.

---

# 1. Company

**Name:** Shifra Nuha Technologies

**Initial geographic market:** All of Kerala, India.

### Core positioning

> **Starting a Business? Get Your Registration & Professional Support in One Place.**

The exact headline can be refined by the AI agent, but the primary message must immediately communicate that Shifra Nuha Technologies helps people start and formalize businesses.

# 5. Target Customers

There are two major audiences.

## Audience A — New businesses

People planning to start a business and needing company incorporation, LLP, partnership, GST, accounting, project reports, trademark or tax support.

Potential business types include small businesses, startups, traders, restaurants, shops, service businesses, agencies, freelancers transitioning into formal businesses, e-commerce businesses, professional businesses, small manufacturers and family businesses.

## Audience B — Existing businesses

Businesses that already operate but need GST, accounting, bookkeeping, income-tax support, audit, project reports, trademark, or a new company/LLP structure.

The website should make it clear that customers do not have to be completely new businesses.
