# Shifra Nuha Technologies — Website Redesign Brief

## 1. Objective

Redesign the existing Shifra Nuha Technologies website to look like a **modern, trustworthy professional business-services firm**, not an AI/SaaS startup.

The primary goal of the website is to convert traffic from Facebook/Instagram ads into **WhatsApp enquiries for LLP and Company registration**.

Our main acquisition offer is:

> **LLP & Company Registration — Starting at ₹2,999 + applicable charges**

This registration service is the entry point. After acquiring the customer, we may cross-sell other services such as GST, accounting, bookkeeping, trademark, websites, automation, etc.

The website should therefore prioritize:

1. Registration enquiries
2. WhatsApp conversations
3. Trust and credibility
4. Clear pricing
5. Simple explanation of the process
6. Secondary presentation of other services

---

# 2. Important Design Direction

Completely remove the generic "AI-generated website" aesthetic.

Do NOT make this look like:

* An AI startup
* A SaaS dashboard
* A futuristic technology company
* A crypto/Web3 website
* A generic startup landing page

Avoid:

* Excessive gradients
* Glowing backgrounds
* Neon effects
* Glassmorphism
* Floating abstract blobs
* Excessive rounded cards
* Huge gradient typography
* 3D AI illustrations
* Robot/AI imagery
* Excessive animations
* Decorative elements that don't communicate anything

Instead, make it feel like:

> **A trustworthy, modern professional business-services company.**

Think:

**Professional + Clean + Affordable + Reliable + Local + Modern**

The visitor should immediately feel:

> "These people can handle my business registration without making the process complicated."

---

# 3. Target Audience

Primary audience:

* People starting a new business in Kerala
* First-time entrepreneurs
* Small-business owners
* Freelancers formalizing their business
* People interested in LLP registration
* People interested in Private Limited/company registration
* New founders
* Small partnerships

Therefore the website must be **mobile-first**.

# 6. Pricing Presentation

The ₹2,999 price is a major selling point.

Make it visually prominent, but do not make the website look like a cheap discount website.

Use wording such as:

> **Starting at ₹2,999 + applicable charges**

Avoid misleading wording such as:

> "Everything for only ₹2,999"

unless that is factually true.

Include a small "What's included?" section.

Example:

### What we help with

* Documentation guidance
* Application preparation
* Registration process assistance
* Communication/support during the process
* Status updates

Only include items that the business actually provides.

Do not invent guarantees, timelines, government approvals, or inclusions.

# 16. Visual Design System

## Overall style

Clean, premium, professional and approachable.

The site should look trustworthy enough that someone would feel comfortable sending business documents through WhatsApp.

## Colors

Use a restrained palette.

Recommended direction:

* Warm/off-white background
* Very dark charcoal/navy text
* One strong brand accent
* Very light neutral backgrounds for sections
* Green can be used selectively for WhatsApp CTA elements

Do not use multiple bright colors.

Do not make every section a different color.

## Typography

Use a professional modern sans-serif.

Suggested fonts:

* Inter
* Manrope
* Plus Jakarta Sans
* DM Sans

Choose ONE primary font family.

Use a clear hierarchy:

* Large bold hero heading
* Medium section headings
* Comfortable body text
* Small supporting text

Do not use excessively huge typography.

---

# 17. Cards

Reduce the number of cards.

Cards should only be used when they improve organization.

Avoid:

* 10+ floating cards
* excessive rounded rectangles
* cards inside cards
* huge shadows
* glass effects

Use subtle borders and very light shadows.

---

# 18. Border Radius

Use moderate border radius.

Buttons:

8–12px

Cards:

12–16px

Do not make everything pill-shaped.

Pill-shaped UI should be reserved for small badges or tags.

---

# 19. Images

Do NOT use generic AI-generated technology images.

Prefer:

* Professional business imagery
* Entrepreneurs
* Small businesses
* Office/business documentation
* Kerala business context where appropriate

If suitable high-quality imagery isn't available, a clean typography/layout-based design is better than using random stock images.

---

# 20. Animations

Keep animations subtle.

Allowed:

* Small hover transitions
* Button hover
* Fade-in sections
* Very subtle entrance animations

Avoid:

* Flying cards
* Excessive parallax
* Glowing animations
* Constant movement
* Text scrambling
* Excessive scroll animations

The website should feel stable and professional.

---

# 21. Mobile-First Requirements

Most advertising traffic will likely come from mobile.

Check carefully at:

* 320px
* 375px
* 390px
* 414px
* 768px
* Desktop widths

Requirements:

* No horizontal scrolling
* Buttons must be easy to tap
* Price must be immediately visible
* WhatsApp CTA must be easy to find
* Hero must not take excessive screen height
* Text must remain readable
* Forms must be simple
* Navigation must work correctly

The WhatsApp button should open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.

Suggested message:

"Hi, I'm interested in LLP/Company registration. I saw your ₹2,999 offer and would like to know more."

Encode the message correctly in the WhatsApp URL.

Do not expose a fake phone number. Use the actual configured business WhatsApp number from the project's environment/configuration.

# 24. Meta Ads Compatibility

The website will receive visitors from Facebook/Instagram ads.

Therefore the landing page must match the ad.

If the ad says:

> LLP & Company Registration from ₹2,999 + applicable charges

**Ad → Landing Page → WhatsApp**

experience.

# 26. Technical Requirements

The existing application is:

* React
* TypeScript
* Vite
* Tailwind CSS

Preserve the existing stack unless there is a strong technical reason to change it.

Do not rewrite the application unnecessarily.

Before changing anything:

1. Inspect the existing component structure.
2. Identify reusable components.
3. Identify the existing routing.
4. Identify existing WhatsApp/contact configuration.
5. Identify existing responsive styles.
6. Identify existing assets.
7. Preserve working functionality.

Then redesign the UI.

Do not break existing functionality.

---

# 29. Important Copy Rule

Do not invent business claims.

Never create:

* Fake customer numbers
* Fake testimonials
* Fake ratings
* Fake reviews
* Fake years of experience
* Fake certifications
* Fake government partnerships
* Fake success rates
* Fake processing times
* Fake guarantees

Use placeholders where information is missing.

For example:

[ADD REAL CUSTOMER TESTIMONIAL]

rather than inventing one.

---

# 30. Do Not Overdesign

The final result should feel like a website built by a good professional designer, not a showcase of every frontend animation available.

The goal is:

**Trust → Clarity → WhatsApp enquiry**

not:

**Wow → Animation → Complexity**
